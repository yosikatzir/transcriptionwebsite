Speakers

Instructions:
1. Add/edit speaker names
2. Click to select a speaker
3. Highlight text in editor
4. Click "Assign to Speaker"

Tip: Click speaker names to edit them
How to use: Upload your diarized JSON transcription and audio file. The transcription will display with existing speaker assignments. Click any text to jump to that timestamp in the audio. The currently playing phrase will be highlighted in yellow. To reassign speakers: select text, choose a speaker from the sidebar, and click "Assign to Speaker". Edit speaker names by clicking them in the sidebar. Edit transcription text directly by clicking it.
